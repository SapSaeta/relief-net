# Hackathon rules (summary)

- Public repo. Team of up to 4 (solo allowed).
- Only new work done during the event (Oct 27 – Nov 4); the demo must show only that.
- Must use Meta models: Muse Spark/Wonder/Image/Voice Transcribe, SAM 3.1 (Meta Model API) or open weights (Muse Glimmer, Muse Spark Open).
- Submission: public repo + 3-min video + live product, before Nov 4 9:00 PM ET (03:00 Nov 5 Madrid).
- Forbidden projects: AI medical advice, basic RAG, simple image analyzers, simple educational chatbots, job screeners, nutrition coaches, personality analyzers, sports, politics or war.
- Phase 1 is pass/fail; phase 2 scores 0–10 on Impact, Use of Meta tools, Technical depth and Demo (equal weight); top 5 per track go to the final.
- Bonus prizes: Most Viral ($20k, X), Open Weights ($10k), Community Vote ($10k), Best Agentic ($5k), Best Creative ($5k, Muse Image/Wonder).
- Credits: $150 Meta (card required; claim before Nov 4 9 PM ET). Baseten/Modal/Fireworks separately.
- Anything shared is not confidential; the organizers receive a broad license.
